# LEGORO Design System

## Direction

A local engineering office: precise, human and grounded. Navigation is user-approved. The local content pass includes the photographic hero, seven readable service rows, regional introduction and a real-team section. Contact framing remains transitional. The footer is a compact logo, wrapping section/privacy links and subtle copyright row; centered and stacked with small gaps on mobile. No map or location block.

## Color

Preserve the actual LR logo and navy/silver identity. Existing brand anchors: navy #041d44, deep navy #031634, silver #B0BEC5. The hero retains the original city photograph with a functional dark contrast overlay and light text. Services use a cool light surface. No beige, decorative glow or glass.

## Typography

Two existing local variable fonts: Plus Jakarta Sans for body/navigation and Space Grotesk for display. Legacy font utility names alias these families to keep untouched sections stable. New headings use sentence case, upright weight and balanced wrapping; body copy stays comfortably readable.

## Layout

Solid navy navigation, clear logo and restrained quote action. The opening preserves the original photo-backed visual identity, centered on mobile and left-aligned on desktop, with the approved energy-efficiency and electrical-safety headline. Do not use a team triptych in the hero. Services are seven confirmed offerings displayed as readable ruled text rows, not icon cards. A short regional introduction preserves the elegirnos anchor without duplicating the team biography. The team section uses photo-dominant navy portrait cards closer to the first published treatment, framing complete heads through the chest in 4:3 photo areas with object-cover/top, following the user reference (not full-body or face-only crops), plus names, roles and a compact LinkedIn action, the confirmed engineering roles and shared UNMdP background; no decorative count or generic expert labels. On mobile, content follows one reading order with no horizontal overflow.

## Components

Use real local portraits with native Astro optimization, descriptive alt text and reserved dimensions. Browser favicons use the existing LR logo mark without the small wordmark, exported as a 256px PNG and multi-size ICO. Preserve existing anchors/menu controls, form island, actual contact details, privacy and branded fixed WhatsApp link. Controls have visible keyboard focus and at least 44px targets. The exact contact heading Consultanos stays on one line with container-relative type sizing, normal word breaking and no hyphenation.

## Motion

No decorative entrance choreography or scaling controls in the new opening. Retain the global reduced-motion safeguard. Later sections will be redesigned separately.
