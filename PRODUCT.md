# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

People seeking energy efficiency, electrical safety, electrical installations, energy or automation services. LEGORO works from Mar del Plata on projects in the city and other localities. No single customer segment has been prioritized.

## Product Purpose

Help visitors understand LEGORO's actual services and contact its real team about a project. This is a family project being developed for the user's brother, with room to grow through clearer service and project content later.

## Brand Personality

Precise, approachable, grounded. Convey the confidence of a local engineering office, not the spectacle of a technology startup.

## Anti-references

Generic AI startup landing pages: repeated icon cards, glass pills, decorative glow, oversized italic slogans, invented statistics and interchangeable marketing copy. Avoid replacing this with a beige editorial template.

## Design Principles

- Lead with energy efficiency and electrical safety, the actual work and regional coverage.
- All team members are Ingeniero Electromecánico. Describe their shared background as ingenieros electromecánicos de la Universidad Nacional de Mar del Plata (UNMdP), without adding credentials or personal biographies.
- Use only the seven confirmed services in lib/data.ts, including the user-authorized provisional electrical consulting description.
- No speed/24-hour promises, technology boasts, a medida claims, signing authority or decorative metrics.
- Use natural professional Spanish with the existing voseo. Keep approved facts and legal meaning; avoid stock filler, forced contrasts and em/en-dash prose separators, including titles.
- Future project content: Sierra is completed solar work and Ayacucho is in progress, but details/photos are pending. Do not create empty routes or fabricated project content.
- Let real people and useful information build trust. Show complete heads through the chest in integrated dark cards, matching the user reference, not full-body portraits or face-only white business cards. Keep the footer compact and centered on mobile, with no map.
- Preserve the existing navy/silver LR identity and original city-photograph hero. The user approves the simplified navigation and rejects the light portrait-triptych hero. Keep portraits in the team section, not the hero.
- Give related services structure rather than identical boxes.
- Grow in small reviewed slices; do not publish a local redesign without authorization.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use readable text, visible keyboard focus, semantic headings, mobile-safe layouts and reduced-motion support. Preserve static contact information and the existing form/CAPTCHA behavior. No additional audience-specific accessibility requirements have been identified.
